1. What the app is

AlbaCam is a vintage film camera for iPhone. It has 62 built-in cameras — film, instant, digital, black-and-white, creative and video — that render their look live in the viewfinder, with a date stamp, frames, light leaks, dust, grain, a timer, flash, double exposure, video recording, developing photos and videos from your library, and an in-app album. All of this is free and works without an internet connection.

One optional feature needs the internet and the AlbaCam Pro subscription: AI cameras. You describe a camera, or name a camera, film or era, and our server asks an AI model to build a camera preset — colour, grain, halation, date stamp, frame, format and icon — which the app renders live and can save for you. AlbaCam Pro is the only thing in the app that costs money.

What the app keeps on your device and what AI cameras send is described in the Privacy Policy.

As between you and us, we claim no rights in your descriptions or in the presets generated for you. You give us permission to process your description only to build the camera and run the service as described in the Privacy Policy.
