Privacy Policy

What stays on your iPhone, and the little that leaves it

Effective date: 1 November 2026. App: AlbaCam for iPhone (iOS 17.0 or later), bundle id com.alba.AlbaCam, Apple ID 6829254633, version 1.0 and later, including the optional AlbaCam Pro subscription. Publisher and data controller: Akhmadiev Albert IE, an individual entrepreneur registered in the Republic of Armenia ("we", "us"). Russian version: privacy-ru.html.

The app's interface is in Russian, so on this page its buttons and switches are quoted in Russian, with a translation where it helps.

The short version

  • AlbaCam is a camera. The camera, the microphone and your photos are processed only on your iPhone. Photos and videos you take or import are never uploaded — not to us and not to anyone else.
  • The in-app album lives on your device, inside the app's own storage. Saving to Photos is add-only: the app can add pictures to your library but cannot see or read it.
  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no tracking. The app contains no third-party SDK at all. We do not sell data.
  • One optional feature uses the internet: AI cameras, part of AlbaCam Pro. When you ask for one, the app sends only the text you typed and Apple-signed data about your subscription to our server in the United Kingdom. The server passes the text — and only the text — to Google (Gemini API, USA) to build the camera preset. No photo, video, viewfinder frame or location is ever sent.
  • Our server does not keep your description, your name or your e-mail. It keeps keyed hashes of Apple transaction identifiers, a record of each generation for 30 days (fair-use limits), the finished camera for up to 72 hours (safe retries) and refund records.
  • AI cameras you save stay on your device. Apple handles all payments; we never see payment details.

1. What stays on your device

Camera and microphone

The viewfinder, the film look (colour, grain, halation, light leaks, dust, date stamp, frame) and recording are all rendered on the iPhone in real time. The microphone is used only to record sound for video, and only if you allow it. Nothing from the camera or the microphone is sent anywhere, and the app does not analyse faces or recognise people.

The album

Photos and videos you take or develop from your library are saved in the app's private storage on your iPhone, together with a thumbnail and a small record: which camera made the frame, when, and how long a video is. Deleting a frame in the app removes it from the album; copies already saved to Photos stay there. Deleting the app deletes the album, and we have no copy. Like any app data, the album can be included in your own iCloud or computer backup of the iPhone if you have backups turned on; that backup is between you and Apple, and we have no access to it.

Saving to Photos

With «Сохранять в Фото» ("Save to Photos") switched on — it is on by default — a copy of each frame is added to your Photos library. The app asks iOS only for add-only access: it can add new pictures but cannot see, read or change the photos you already have. You can turn the switch off in the app's settings, or change the access in iOS Settings → AlbaCam → Photos.

Importing from Photos

«Импорт» opens the iOS photo picker. The picker runs outside the app, and the app receives only the photo or video you pick. It applies the look on the device and saves the result to the album (and to Photos, if that switch is on). To print the right date on an imported picture, the app reads the capture date stored in the file, on the device. The app never asks for access to your whole photo library.

Sharing

«Поделиться» ("Share") opens the iOS share sheet. What you share goes only where you choose to send it, under the terms of that app or service. AlbaCam itself sends nothing.

Settings

Stored in the standard iOS settings storage (UserDefaults): the selected camera, each camera's options (date stamp, light leak, dust, frame, grain, strength, frame format), your favourite cameras, the switches for saving to Photos, mirroring the front camera, the grid and haptics, a custom date for the date stamp, the timer and the flash. For AlbaCam Pro the app also keeps your choice on the AI consent screen, your App Store country (to hide Pro where it is not offered), whether a purchase is waiting for approval, and a count of app sessions with the time the Pro reminder was last shown, so that the reminder stays rare. While an AI camera is being built, the app keeps your description and the random request number, so that an interrupted request can be finished without being counted twice; this entry is deleted once the request finishes and is never used after 72 hours. None of this contains your name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, location or any advertising identifier, and none of it is sent anywhere except the description and request number described in section 2.

Your AI cameras

A camera you save with «Сохранить и снимать» ("Save and shoot") is stored on your device — its name, tagline and look settings — and is listed under «Мои» ("Mine") in the camera catalog, where you can rename or delete it. Saved cameras are never uploaded or synced, and they keep working after a subscription ends.

2. AI cameras: what is sent, to whom, and why

When the app contacts our server

Only when you tap «Собрать камеру» ("Build camera") or «Ещё вариант» ("Another take") in the AI studio, or when the app repeats a request you started (for example after the connection dropped). Opening the AI studio, the catalog or the AlbaCam Pro screen does not contact our server; the Pro screen asks only Apple for prices. If you never create an AI camera, the app never contacts our server.

Your consent comes first

Before the first description leaves your iPhone, the app shows a consent screen that names our server and Google Gemini (USA) and asks for your agreement. You agree by tapping «Согласиться и собрать» ("Agree and build"). You can withdraw it at any time with the «Описания для ИИ» ("Descriptions for AI") switch in the «AlbaCam Pro» section of the app's settings; after that, no description is sent.

What our server receives

  • Your description (3 to 300 characters) and the language for the answer (Russian or English). Describe a camera, a film or a look — not a person. Please do not include personal information about yourself or anyone else.
  • Apple-signed subscription transactions. They contain the plan, purchase and expiry dates, Apple transaction identifiers, the App Store country and whether it is a Sandbox (test) purchase. They contain no name, no e-mail, no Apple Account details and no payment details. The server checks Apple's signature to confirm that AlbaCam Pro is active.
  • A random request number created by the app, so that a repeated request is not run or counted twice.
  • Your IP address, as with any internet connection. The server holds it only briefly in memory to limit how many requests one address can send (20 a minute). It is not written to the server's database.

What Google receives

Our server sends only your description and the language, wrapped in our own instructions, to the Gemini API of Google LLC (USA). Google does not receive any identifier, purchase data or your IP address — the request comes from our server. Google processes the text under its Gemini API terms to return the camera preset. Our AI gateway, which runs on the same server, records only technical usage (model, token counts, cost), not the text. We do not use your descriptions to train AI models.

What comes back

A camera preset: a name, a short tagline and numbers for colour, grain, halation, frame, date stamp and the like. Your iPhone renders it live in the viewfinder. Nothing from the camera ever travels the other way.

Where the server is

A virtual server rented from AEZA in London, United Kingdom, set up and maintained by the developer.

4. Purchases and Apple

AlbaCam Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and runs your Apple Account under its own privacy policy. We never receive your name, e-mail, Apple Account details or payment details. Apple also sends our server notifications about subscription events, such as renewals and refunds. The server checks their signature and keeps only the refund and revocation records described above.

5. What the app does not access or do

  • No location: the app has no location permission and adds no location to your pictures. The date stamp uses the iPhone's clock or a date you choose.
  • No contacts, calendars, health data, Bluetooth or local network.
  • No reading of your photo library — only add-only saving and the system picker described above.
  • No advertising identifier, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and therefore no tracking permission prompt.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK. If you have turned on "Share with App Developers" in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share crash reports and usage diagnostics with us in a form Apple prepares; that is controlled by iOS, not by the app.

6. Who receives data

Only those without whom AI cameras cannot work: Google (Gemini API) receives the text of your descriptions, and AEZA hosts our server in the United Kingdom. Apple processes purchases. Data is not sold or rented, and it is not given to ad networks or data brokers. We disclose data only when the law requires it.

This means that, when you use AI cameras, data leaves your country: our server is in the United Kingdom and Google processes the text in the United States.

7. Legal bases (UK and EU users)

We process this data to create the AI camera you ask for and to provide the subscription you bought (performance of a contract), and to keep the service fair and safe — rate and fair-use limits, refunds, abuse prevention (legitimate interests). The consent screen shown before the first description is an additional safeguard on top of that.

8. Retention, deletion and your choices

  • If you never create an AI camera, nothing about you reaches our server.
  • You can withdraw your AI consent at any time with the «Описания для ИИ» switch in the «AlbaCam Pro» section of the app's settings.
  • You can delete AI cameras under «Мои» in the catalog and frames in the album. Deleting the app erases everything it stored on the device. We hold no copies of your photos, videos or cameras and cannot restore them.
  • Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel it in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Data on our server is deleted on the schedule above. To ask what we hold or to ask for deletion, write to us (section 11). The server does not know your name or e-mail, so we usually cannot find records by them; tell us what you need and we will explain what is possible. We may keep refund records, because without them refunded subscriptions would keep working.
  • If you are in the UK or the EU, you may also complain to a data-protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

9. Children

AlbaCam is rated 4+ in the App Store and is a general-audience camera; it is not directed at children. AlbaCam Pro is bought through an Apple Account, so for children's accounts purchases follow the family's Apple settings, such as Ask to Buy. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. An AI camera description should describe a look, not a person; if you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will help delete it.
