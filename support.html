Support

Help with AlbaCam

Support for AlbaCam on iPhone (iOS 17.0 or later). Effective date: 1 November 2026.

The app's interface is in Russian, so buttons and messages are quoted below in Russian, with a translation. If the answer is not here, write to us.

Try this first

  • Update the app. Open the App Store, tap your profile picture and check for updates. The app needs iOS 17.0 or later.
  • Close and reopen it. Swipe AlbaCam away in the app switcher and open it again. If that does not help, restart the iPhone.
  • The camera works offline. All 62 built-in cameras, video, import, the album, the timer, flash and double exposure work without the internet. Only AI cameras need a connection.

Camera, microphone and Photos

  • «Камере нужен доступ» ("The camera needs access"). Open iOS Settings → AlbaCam and turn on Camera. The button «Открыть Настройки» in the app takes you there.
  • A video has no sound. The app asks for the microphone the first time you pick a video camera. If you declined, turn on Microphone in iOS Settings → AlbaCam. The microphone is used only for video sound.
  • «Сохранено в альбом, но не в Фото» ("Saved to the album, but not to Photos"). Photos access is off. In iOS Settings → AlbaCam → Photos choose "Add Photos Only" — that is all the app needs; it never reads your library. Or switch off «Сохранять в Фото» in the app's settings if you want to keep frames only in the album.
  • «Камера занята другим приложением» ("The camera is busy"). Close the other app using the camera (a video call, for example) and come back.
  • «Телефон перегрелся — камера временно отключена» ("The phone overheated"). iOS pauses the camera to cool down. Let the iPhone rest for a few minutes.
  • «Не удалось сохранить файл — возможно, на устройстве мало места» ("Could not save — storage may be full"). Free some space in iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage.

The album

  • Where are my frames? In the in-app album (the button next to the shutter) and, with «Сохранять в Фото» on, also in Photos. Frames never leave the iPhone and are not stored by us.
  • Deleting. Deleting a frame in AlbaCam removes it from the album only; its copy in Photos stays.
  • Reinstalling. Deleting the app deletes the album. We have no copy, so save anything you want to keep to Photos first.

AlbaCam Pro

  • What it is. An auto-renewable subscription that unlocks one thing: creating your own cameras with AI. Everything else in the app is free. Two plans unlock exactly the same: Pro Monthly («Pro на месяц») and Pro Yearly («Pro на год»). In the US they cost $2.99 a month or $14.99 a year; elsewhere the App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy. There is no free trial.
  • How to subscribe. Tap the PRO chip at the top of the camera screen or the «ИИ» tile at the start of the camera strip, choose a plan and tap «Оформить Pro» ("Get Pro").
  • How to restore purchases. On a new iPhone or after reinstalling, tap «Восстановить покупки» ("Restore purchases") on the AlbaCam Pro screen or in the app's settings, section «AlbaCam Pro». Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with, and make sure it is signed in to the App Store (iOS Settings → your name → Media & Purchases).
  • How to manage or cancel. In the app: settings → «AlbaCam Pro» → «Управлять подпиской» ("Manage subscription"). Or on the iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → AlbaCam Pro. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Pro works until the end of the paid period. Turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.
  • The purchase is "pending". With Ask to Buy, a family organiser has to approve the purchase. Pro switches on by itself once Apple confirms it.
  • Refunds. Payments are processed by Apple, and only Apple can issue a refund: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Family Sharing is not available for AlbaCam Pro.

AI cameras

  • How to create one. Tap the «ИИ» tile at the start of the camera strip (or the «Создать камеру с ИИ» card at the top of the catalog). Describe a camera in your own words or name one — "a warm 90s point-and-shoot with flash", a film stock, an era — and tap «Собрать камеру» ("Build camera"). The first time, the app asks for your consent to send the description to our server and Google Gemini: tap «Согласиться и собрать» ("Agree and build"). It usually takes up to half a minute. When the camera is ready you see it live on your viewfinder: tap «Сохранить и снимать» ("Save and shoot") or «Ещё вариант» ("Another take").
  • Where saved cameras live. In the catalog, tab «Мои» ("Mine"): rename, delete or add to favourites. You can keep up to 50; delete one to make room. They stay on your iPhone and keep working after Pro ends.
  • Consent. Only the text of your description is sent — never photos or videos. To stop sending descriptions, switch off «Описания для ИИ» in the app's settings → «AlbaCam Pro».
  • Limits. Up to 10 AI cameras a day (the day resets at midnight UTC) and 50 in any 30 days, per subscriber; one request at a time. Every accepted request counts, including a refused one and «Ещё вариант». When a limit is reached the app says «Лимит Pro: 10 камер в день и 50 за 30 дней» and when new ones are possible. Many requests in a row from one connection can show «Слишком много попыток подряд» ("Too many attempts in a row"); wait a minute.
  • «Опишите камеру подробнее» ("Describe the camera in more detail"). A description must be 3 to 300 characters.
  • The AI declined. The request was not about a camera or photo look, or asked for content the rules do not allow (sexual, violent or hateful content, real private people). Edit the description and try again.
  • «ИИ-камеры временно недоступны» ("AI cameras are temporarily unavailable"). The service is paused for maintenance or because demand is unusually high. The rest of the app works as usual; please try again later.
  • No connection or a timeout. AI cameras need the internet. Retrying is safe: a retried request is not counted twice.
  • Why AI cameras are unavailable in some regions. AlbaCam Pro and AI cameras are not offered in mainland China, so the PRO chip and the «ИИ» tile are hidden when your Apple Account's App Store country is mainland China. This follows the App Store country of the account (iOS Settings → your name → Media & Purchases → View Account → Country/Region), not where you are. Pro can be bought only through the App Store; if your Apple Account cannot make App Store purchases in your country, it cannot be bought there.
  • The result does not look like the camera I named. A generated camera is the AI's interpretation and can be imperfect. Add details — colours, era, grain, flash, date stamp — or ask for another take.
  • A generated name or tagline is inappropriate. Tap «Сообщить о проблеме» (Report a problem) under the new camera or in the «…» menu of its tile in the «Мои» tab: it opens an e-mail to us with the name and tagline. Add the description you used if you like, so we can improve the rules. You can delete the camera from the same menu.

How to reach us

  • E-mail: InnoAppDesign@yandex.com
  • GitHub issues: github.com/aakhmadievvll-cyber/AlbaCam-Legal/issues

What to put in a report

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About) and the app version.
  • Which camera was selected and what you did, step by step.
  • The exact message on screen, or a screenshot of it.
  • For AI cameras: the description you typed and roughly when (with time zone).

Keep private information out of GitHub. Issues are public. Do not post photos of people, your Apple Account e-mail, receipts or order numbers there; send those by e-mail if they are really needed.

Privacy requests

AlbaCam keeps your photos, videos, album and saved cameras only on your iPhone; we never receive them. Our server keeps only what AI cameras need, for the periods in the Privacy Policy. To ask what we hold or to request deletion, e-mail us. The server does not know your name or e-mail, so tell us what you need and we will explain what is possible.
